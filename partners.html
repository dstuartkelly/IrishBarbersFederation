PARTNERS & SPONSORS

Building the future
of barbering, together.

Barbering is stronger when the people around it pull in the same direction. The IBF hopes to work with businesses and educators who care about the craft.

Who we'd like to work with

  • Product suppliers and manufacturers that serve the trade;
  • Colleges, training providers and other educators;
  • Businesses offering services that help barbers and barbershops, such as insurance and finance;
  • Other organisations that share our commitment to the profession.

Partnering with the IBF

We want partnerships that are open, fair and good for both barbers and partners. We are still shaping how partnerships will work, and we will share more as that develops.
