HOW IT WORKS

What is live,
what is planned.

Upgrading

Standard members will be able to apply for the licence once the criteria and the application process are published. You will not need to rejoin.

Assessment

Licence applications are intended to be assessed against published requirements, with a reasoned decision and a fair route to ask for a review. We intend to include a route for experienced barbers who do not hold formal qualifications. Details are subject to IBF approval and are not yet published.

Renewal

The licence is intended to renew every year, alongside membership.

Status of each feature

  • Opening soon Standard Membership enrolment
  • Planned Licence assessment and application
  • Planned Digital licence and public verification
  • Exploring Insurance and commercial partner benefits, only after written agreements are in place

Please note. The IBF Professional Barber Licence is a voluntary IBF credential. It is not a statutory licence to practise barbering in Ireland, it is not Government authorisation or recognition, it is not insurance or proof of cover, and it is not a guarantee of competence.
