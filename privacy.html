PRIVACY NOTICE

Your data,
handled with care.

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This notice explains what personal data the Irish Barbering Federation (IBF) collects when you register your interest in IBF 2000, and what we do with it.

Who we are

The IBF is the controller of your personal data. [LEGAL ENTITY NAME AND REGISTERED ADDRESS TO CONFIRM]. You can contact us about your data at info@barbersfederationofireland.com.

What we collect

When you complete the register-your-interest form we collect:

  • your name and email address (required);
  • your phone number and years of experience (optional);
  • your IP address and browser details (user agent), recorded automatically when you submit the form;
  • the date and time of your registration and your consent.

Why we use it

  • To keep a register of interest in IBF 2000, so we can tell you when enrolment opens and plan the campaign. Our lawful basis is your consent.
  • To send you updates about developments at the IBF, including IBF 2000, by email. Our lawful basis is your consent.
  • To keep the form secure and prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, using your IP address and browser details. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in protecting the site.

Registering your interest is free and does not commit you to joining. We do not sell your data.

Who we share it with

Your details are stored in a database on a server provided by Blacknight in Ireland. The IBF administers the database; Blacknight only provides the server. We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it. If we later use an email service to send updates, we will name it here.

How long we keep it

We keep your details until you withdraw your consent or ask us to delete them. We review the register every 24 months. Security logs such as IP address are kept for the same period as your registration.

Your rights

Under data protection law you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to its use, and receive a copy in a portable format. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting what we did before. Every update email will include an unsubscribe option, or you can email us and we will remove you.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

The registration form uses a single session cookie to protect against cross-site request forgery. It is strictly necessary and is not used for tracking. This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your IP address is sent to Google when a page loads.

Changes to this notice

If we change how we use your data we will update this page and, where the change matters, tell you by email.
